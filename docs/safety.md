# Safety and validation checklist

- [ ] Verify stable base and tip resistance for the intended use.
- [ ] Account for full-height lever effects and movement of upper components.
- [ ] Provide reachable emergency stop / power isolation.
- [ ] Secure and protect electrical wiring, batteries and moving mechanisms.
- [ ] Test motors initially with no exposed moving mechanism and appropriate adult or lab supervision.
- [ ] Define maximum test load based on calculations and validation, not guesswork.
- [ ] Do not operate the device near others until basic mechanical and electrical risks are addressed.
